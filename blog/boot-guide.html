The Three Boot Types

Trail Runners

Light, flexible, fast-drying.

Best for: Day hikes, dry conditions

Day Hikers

Mid-height, moderate support.

Best for: Weekend trips, uneven terrain

Backpacking Boots

Full support, stiff soles.

Best for: Heavy packs, rough terrain

Frequently Asked Questions

Should I buy boots a size larger?

Yes, generally go up half to a full size.

Are waterproof boots worth it?

For wet climates, yes. For dry, non-waterproof breathes better.

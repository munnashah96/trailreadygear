How TrailReady Gear Started

It was 2016, and I was halfway up Mt. Whitney with a pack that weighed 45 pounds. My back ached, my feet were blistered, and I kept thinking: "There has to be a better way."

That trip changed everything. I started obsessively researching gear—weighing every item, reading forums, testing different setups. Over the next few years, I shaved 20 pounds off my pack and discovered the joy of moving fast and light.

Friends kept asking for gear advice. Then strangers. Then I realized: the outdoor industry is full of marketing hype, but there's no single place for real, unbiased gear data from actual hikers. So I started TrailReady Gear.

Today, we're a small team of passionate backpackers, climbers, and trail runners who test gear year-round in conditions ranging from the desert southwest to the Pacific Northwest rainforests.
